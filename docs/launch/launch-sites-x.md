# Niche launch sites with a fast free option (X focus)

Research date: 2026-10-23. Every fact below was checked on the site's own pages on that date unless marked **unverified**. X follower counts come from public profile mirrors (api.fxtwitter.com, superx.so) on the same date, because x.com needs a login and Nitter is offline. Traffic figures are the sites' own claims unless a source says otherwise; no independent (Similarweb style) figure was found for any of them.

Left out as already known: Product Hunt, Uneed, Peerlist Launchpad, BetaList, Fazier, TinyLaunch, MicroLaunch, DevHunt, Indie Hackers, Hacker News, Pitchwall/Betapage, Twelve Tools, SideProjectors, Tiny Startups, AlternativeTo, SaaSHub, There's An AI For That, Softonic, Uptodown, MacUpdate, FileHorse.

Note on the brief: it says Uneed's free line put us in "March 2026", which is already past on the research date. It is read here as March 2027; either way, long free queues are excluded.

## Headline findings

- No niche launch site has real X reach. The biggest accounts are Launching Next (14.9k followers, but its auto posts get 17 to 55 views and 0 likes) and StartupBase (3.3k). Most sit between 100 and 1,700 followers, and their posts get tens to a few hundred views. The real X reach is our own launch post; these sites are mostly for listings, backlinks and a few clicks.
- Mac-specific directories (MacMenuBar, MacNative, awesome-mac) fit far better than general boards and are free with short or no queues.
- Almost every fast free path needs a badge or backlink on shepetal.app. Site rule: nothing may load from another host, so any badge must be a self-hosted static image or a plain text link, and the homepage change is Pavel's call.
- Maker launch posts tagging these sites: searches surfaced too little to measure engagement for any site. Treat "makers post here" as **unverified** across the board.
- Voting: we never ask for upvotes. ScrollLaunch, Smol Launch, LaunchIgniter and PeerPush ban vote rings, swaps, incentivised votes and multiple accounts in their terms. Our X post links to the listing and asks people to take a look, nothing more.

## All candidates checked

### Mac-specific

**MacNative**: https://macnative.io, submit https://macnative.io/submit
- Free: "every app is welcome for free": listing page, dofollow backlink, basic analytics, badge, standard review queue. Review is "by hand… can take a little while"; wait **unverified**. Pricing: https://macnative.io/pricing
- Paid: Pro $19 per app per month (priority review, homepage highlight, newsletter and social promotion, a spot on the awesome-native-macosx-apps list). SEO article $50.
- Requirements: native code (Swift, AppKit); browser wrappers rejected (https://macnative.io/guidelines). Maintained app, clear pricing. Paid apps listed (Things, Orca One one-time). Direct sale (outside the Mac App Store) not addressed; shepetal is native Swift, so it fits.
- Weekly launches ranked by upvotes; top 3 get a badge and a shoutout on its X and newsletter (https://macnative.io/launches). The Oct 19 to 25 leader had 12 upvotes, so top 3 is realistic.
- X: footer links x.com/best_macapps; the handle did not resolve. Followers **unverified**.
- Size: 178 apps listed. Traffic **unverified**.
- Voting rules: none found beyond the upvote ranking.

**MacMenuBar**: https://macmenubar.com, submit https://macmenubar.com/submit-your-menu-bar-app/
- Free: the only way to get listed, "reviewed manually", no stated wait. Recently Added updated 2026-10-23.
- Paid (ads, not a queue skip): Category Pin $15/mo, Sticky Placement $80/mo, Collections Banner $80/mo, Top Banner $125/mo (https://macmenubar.com/sponsorship/).
- Requirements: a true macOS menu bar app. shepetal runs as a menu bar app (LSUIElement), so it fits. Paid direct-sale apps carry a "Paid" tag (Bartender Pro, Copybook).
- Size: 1,600+ app pages, "monthly-ish" newsletter; most visitors come from search per its sponsorship page. Traffic dashboard not readable.
- X: no handle on the site; **unverified**.
- No dictation app in recent additions: the niche looks open. No voting.

**awesome-mac (GitHub)**: https://github.com/jaywcjlove/awesome-mac
- Free PR adding one line to the Voice-to-Text section of all four READMEs (EN, ZH, JA, KO). PR #3751 (Voltip, Voice-to-Text) opened 2026-10-22, merged 2026-10-23.
- 1,416 open PRs, some from 2020, so a merge is not guaranteed. No written paid-app policy; paid dictation apps are listed (Ottex, Spokenly, Willow Voice). 116.8k stars.
- No X presence. Smaller sibling: awesome-native-macosx-apps (1.7k stars, run by MacNative), accepts paid native apps.

**MacIgniter**: https://macigniter.com, submit https://macigniter.com/submit (account)
- Free: "read in the order they arrive, which can take up to 4 weeks", nofollow link, human review.
- Paid: $19 reviewed in minutes with followed link; $29 adds 30 days featured; $49 homepage banner.
- Mac-only fields (price, minimum macOS, Apple silicon). Very new: 60 apps, run by the LaunchIgniter founder. A dictation app (Murmurtale) is listed.
- X: @macigniter, created 2026-09-24, 3 followers. No reach.

**Apps.Deals**: https://apps.deals/submit. Mac apps on discount only (original price, offer price, offer dates). Fits only during a promo. Free or paid for a normal listing and review time **unverified**. X @AppsDeals_ 18 followers; maker @rampatra_ 1,355.

**Indie Mac Apps**: https://indiemacapps.com/submit. Free, editor reviewed (icon + 2 screenshots). No stated wait; homepage timed out. @indiemacapps on X: 1 follower, ownership **unverified**. Very little reach.

### General launch boards and directories

**Launching Next**: https://www.launchingnext.com, submit https://www.launchingnext.com/submit/ (no account)
- Free: "We review submissions every day, and you'll receive an email if we publish your startup." Not guaranteed. 5 to 7 days per third parties (**unverified**).
- Paid: $99 for consideration within 1 business day.
- Requirements: 5 to 8 word headline, description up to 2,500 characters, 5 to 10 tags. No badge, no account.
- X: @LaunchingNext 14,929 followers; auto posts every published startup via IFTTT, 17 to 55 views, 0 likes each.
- Traffic: 45,669 startups featured, newsletter 5,000+ (self-stated). No voting.

**ScrollLaunch**: https://www.scrolllaunch.com, submit /dashboard/products/new
- Free: 25 slots per ISO week, weekly leaderboard; dofollow needs its badge, re-checked weekly. Next free date not published (**unverified**).
- Paid: Premium $39 one-time (https://www.scrolllaunch.com/pricing).
- Activity: week 43 had 32 live products, 393 upvotes; 546 makers total (https://www.scrolllaunch.com/stats).
- X: @scrolllaunch 332 followers; posts "just went live", midweek ranks and weekly leaderboards, 1 to 4 likes, 37 to 389 views.
- Rules: bans rings, swaps, incentivised votes, multiple accounts, reviewing your own product; penalties up to deletion (https://www.scrolllaunch.com/terms).

**Startup Fame**: https://startupfa.me, submit https://startupfa.me/submit (account)
- Free ("Verified"): do-follow listing, page, "Featured" badge. Pricing says "Available 7 days"; FAQ "Review can take several business days."
- Requirement: Startup Fame badge on the homepage, bot-checked before manual review.
- Paid: Highlight $19/mo, Spotlight $149/mo (showed "0 spot left"); both instant, no badge.
- X: @StartupFame 122 followers, 62 posts; engagement **unverified**. Traffic: "10K+ monthly visits" (self-stated). No voting.

**PeerPush**: https://peerpush.com/submit (peerpush.net redirects)
- Free: "Wait in line behind paid users"; queue position shown only after submitting (**unverified**).
- Points route: 400 PeerPush points launch immediately as a Standard launch (https://peerpush.com/faq). Points come from activity on other products, capped about 10 a day each (https://peerpush.com/rules). 8 to 10 days is our estimate. Only do this with genuine use; it must never become vote trading.
- Paid: Standard $39 instant; +7 days promo $89; +30 days $229; ads from $129/week.
- X: @peerpush_com 1,640 followers, 3,509 posts; daily "Best new products" and single product posts, 20 to 150 views, 0 to 6 likes. Featured Sanelo (local dictation, Germany) on 2026-10-21.
- Traffic claim "1.3M+ monthly visits" (internal, likely inflated). Terms ban automated votes and multiple accounts (https://peerpush.com/terms).

**TrustMRR**: https://trustmrr.com ("Add startup" on the homepage; /add returns 404, exact URL **unverified**)
- Free: basic listing $0, instant once a provider is connected (Stripe, LemonSqueezy, Paddle, App Store and others). Paid: dofollow $19, visibility boost $79, sidebar from $399/7 days (https://trustmrr.com/faq).
