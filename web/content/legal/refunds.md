# Refund policy

Last revised on October 22, 2026.

## Try it first

shepetal is free for 30 days. No card. You pay only when you decide to keep it.

## Lifetime license ($23.99 once)

Changed your mind? Write to founder@backant.io within 14 days of your purchase, from the email you signed in with. You get a full refund. The refund ends the license.

## Monthly subscription ($0.99 a month)

Cancel anytime in your account, under Manage subscription. shepetal keeps working until the end of the month you paid for, and nothing more is charged. A month that has started is not refunded.

## Your right of withdrawal

shepetal is digital content, delivered the moment you pay. In the European Union, the United Kingdom and other places with a 14-day right of withdrawal, you ask at checkout for delivery right away and acknowledge that this ends that right. Your receipt confirms it. The 14-day refund on the lifetime license above applies anyway.

## How refunds work

We refund to the card you paid with. Your bank shows it within 5 to 10 business days.

## Your rights

Nothing here limits the rights the law where you live gives you, including your rights if shepetal is faulty.
