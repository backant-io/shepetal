# The 🤫 indicator

While Shepetal runs, a small 🤫 sits in the bottom-left corner of your main display. It stays on top across Spaces and over full-screen apps. Clicks go straight through it, so it never takes focus.

## Colors

| Ring | Meaning |
|---|---|
| No ring | Idle, ready for fn |
| Red | Recording while you hold fn, or a meeting is being recorded |
| Orange | Hands-free recording. Tap fn to stop |
| Pulsing blue | Turning your speech into text |
| Yellow | Something needs your attention. A short message shows next to 🤫 |

Shepetal makes no sounds. The ring is how it tells you what it is doing.

## Messages next to 🤫

Short notes show to the right of 🤫 and then go away, for example:

- "Hold fn to talk · double-tap for hands-free" after a too-short press.
- "No speech heard" when the recording had no words in it.
- A note that the text is on the clipboard, when it could not be pasted. See [When text goes to the clipboard](clipboard).
- A note about your mic, such as "Speak closer or use a wired mic". See [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting).
- "Meeting detected" with **Record** and **Not now**. See [Meetings](meetings).

## The 🤫 menu

The 🤫 in the menu bar opens a short menu. At the top, only when needed: **Permissions needed…**, the speech recognition download, sign-in, and buying. Then:

- **Record meeting** and **Past meetings**
- **Open shepetal**, the Shepetal window with Home, History, Meetings and Settings
- **Paste last transcript** and **Recent**. Click a recent dictation to copy it.
- **Shortcuts…**, **Microphone** and **Languages**
- **Help center** and **Send feedback…**
- **Quit shepetal**

Recordings, Start at login, the dictionary and permissions are in **Open shepetal > Settings**.
