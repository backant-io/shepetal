# When text goes to the clipboard

Shepetal pastes only into the window you started dictating in. It never switches windows back for you. When it can't paste safely, it leaves the text on the clipboard and the note next to 🤫 says to press ⌘V.

## When this happens

- **You moved to another window** while speaking or while the text was being prepared. Click where you want the text and press ⌘V. Moving the cursor within the same window is fine: the text lands at the new spot.
- **The paste didn't land**, for example because no text field had focus.
- **Secure input is on** in another app, such as a password field or Terminal's Secure Keyboard Entry. Shepetal shows a note and pastes nothing. Your text is on the clipboard.

In these cases your previous clipboard is not put back, so ⌘V gives you the dictation.

## When the paste works

Shepetal pastes through the clipboard and then puts back what you had copied before, about half a second later.

## Finding text later

- **⌃⌘V** pastes your last dictation again. **Paste last transcript** in the 🤫 menu does the same.
- **Recent** in the 🤫 menu lists your latest dictations. Click one to copy it.
- **Open shepetal > History** has every dictation, with search and **Copy**.
