# Hold fn to talk

## Hold to talk

Hold fn, speak, and let go. The text is pasted where your cursor is.

## Hands-free

Tap fn twice quickly (the second press within half a second of letting go of the first). The 🤫 ring turns orange and stays on after you let go. Speak as long as you like, then tap fn once to stop.

You can also press **fn+Space** to start hands-free, or to lock a hold you have already started. Press it again to stop.

A dictation stops by itself after 20 minutes and is inserted. "1 minute left" shows a minute before.

## Too-short presses

A press shorter than half a second, with no second tap, inserts nothing. "Hold fn to talk · double-tap for hands-free" shows next to 🤫 for about 2 seconds. The recording is still kept and marked "too short" in your history.

## fn shortcuts still work

If you press another key in the first second of holding fn, such as fn+Delete or fn+arrow keys, Shepetal treats it as a shortcut and cancels the recording. Nothing is inserted. The recording is kept and marked "cancelled".

Once you have held fn for more than a second, other keys no longer cancel the dictation.

## Other shortcuts

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Esc** | Cancels a recording or a transcription. The audio is kept. |
| **⌃⌘V** | Pastes your last dictation again. Also **Paste last transcript** in the 🤫 menu. |
| **⌃⌘C** | Copies your last dictation. |

Choose **Shortcuts…** in the 🤫 menu to change them. Each shortcut can use up to 3 keys and must include a modifier, fn or a mouse button.

## Languages

**Automatic** works for most people. To pick the languages you speak, choose **Languages** in the 🤫 menu.

## How the text fits in

Shepetal adds a space before the text when needed and keeps it lowercase in the middle of a sentence, with names keeping their capital. If you have text selected, the dictation replaces it and keeps its case, end punctuation and spacing.

To teach Shepetal a word, a name or a replacement, open **Open shepetal > Settings > Dictionary…**. Put one entry per line. Write `spoken => written` for a replacement. Changes apply from the next dictation.
