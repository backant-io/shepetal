# Trial and license

## The free trial

Your 30-day free trial starts the first time you sign in to Shepetal, on the website or in the app. You don't need a credit card. You sign in with your Google account.

## After 30 days

**Coming soon:** what the app does when the trial ends. This page will say exactly what changes before trials end.

## Buying

Shepetal costs **$4.99, once**. There is no subscription. Your license never expires, and future updates are included.

You pay through Stripe Checkout. Once the payment goes through, your account is active for good.

## Signing in from the app

**Coming soon.** The app will open shepetal.backant.io in your browser. You sign in with Google there, check the account it names, and click **Continue**. The browser hands you back to the app, and you're signed in. You never type your password into the app.

The app stays signed in as long as you use it. After 90 days without use, you sign in again. **Sign out** in the app ends that sign-in right away.

## A new Mac

Your license belongs to your Google account, not to one Mac. Install Shepetal on the new Mac and sign in with the same Google account.

Your recordings and history stay on the old Mac. They are not part of your account and are never uploaded.
