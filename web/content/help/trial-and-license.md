# Trial and license

## The free trial

Choose **Sign in to start your free trial…** in the 🤫 menu, or **Sign in with Google to start your free 30-day trial** on **Home** in the Shepetal window. A sign-in sheet opens shepetal.backant.io and goes to Google. If your browser is already signed in to Google, one click picks the account. There's nothing to type and no code to copy.

Your 30-day trial starts at your first sign-in, on the website or in the app. You don't need a credit card. The top of **Home** shows who is signed in and how many days are left, for example "Signed in as you@example.com · Trial, 12 days left".

## After 30 days

When the trial ends, dictation stops until you buy. Holding fn shows "Trial ended: get Shepetal for $4.99" next to 🤫 and opens **Home**. Nothing is recorded. Your recordings, history and meetings stay on your Mac.

## Buying

Shepetal costs **$4.99, once**. There is no subscription. Your license never expires, and future updates are included.

Click **Get Shepetal for $4.99** on **Home**. From 7 days left in your trial, the 🤫 menu shows **Get shepetal for $4.99…** too. Checkout opens in your browser through Stripe. Within about a minute of paying, **Home** says "Yours forever".

## Offline

Shepetal checks your license when it starts and every 24 hours, in the background and never while you dictate. The last answer holds for 7 days without an internet connection.

## Signing out

Click **Sign out** on **Home**. The app stays signed in as long as you use it. After 90 days without use, you sign in again.

## A new Mac

Your license belongs to your Google account, not to one Mac. Install Shepetal on the new Mac and sign in with the same Google account.

Your recordings, history and meetings stay on the old Mac. They are not part of your account and are never uploaded.
